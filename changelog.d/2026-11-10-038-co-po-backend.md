# NBA outcomes backend: POs, COs, CO–PO mapping, AI extraction, outcomes report (Phase 6.4)
- Type: feature
- Area: backend (database, AI, API)
- Commit: see `git log --grep "Add CO/PO outcomes backend"`

## Why
Owner decision: NBA accreditation (CO/PO). Design: `_claude/plans/11-co-po-design.md`.

## What changed
- `V6__course_and_program_outcomes.sql`: `program_outcome` (PO/PSO per program),
  `course_outcome` (CO per syllabus subject, optional Bloom level), `co_po_mapping`
  (level 1–3). `SchemaVersionCheck.EXPECTED_VERSION` = 6.
- `OutcomeService` + `OutcomeController` (`/api/outcomes`):
  - `GET /programs/{id}`; `POST /programs/{id}/nba` adds PO1–PO12 (NBA titles; short
    descriptions written for CampusPulse; skips existing codes); `POST/PUT/DELETE
    /programs/{id}[/{poId}]` for POs/PSOs — college admin or the department's HOD.
  - `GET /syllabus/{id}` → program POs + subjects with COs and their CO–PO levels (anyone who
    can read the syllabus).
  - `POST /syllabus/{id}/extract?replace=false` → LLM (JSON mode) reads COs per subject from the
    syllabus text (or writes 4–6 from the units) with Bloom level and suggested PO levels;
    `replace=false` keeps subjects that already have COs. Needs change rights on the syllabus.
  - `POST /subjects/{id}`, `PUT/DELETE /cos/{id}`, `PUT /cos/{id}/mapping {"poId": 0..3}`.
  - `GET /report?syllabusId&jobId` → per CO the job skills it covers (topic → closest CO by
    embeddings) and the GAP skills that fit it best; per PO/PSO coverage % =
    Σ level×(1 matched / 0.5 partial) ÷ Σ level×skills over mapped COs.
- Test: `OutcomeServiceTest` (PO coverage weighting, vector helpers, 12 NBA POs).

## How to test (restart backend with mvnw → Flyway V6)

`GET /api/outcomes/report?syllabusId=<id>&jobId=<id>`.
